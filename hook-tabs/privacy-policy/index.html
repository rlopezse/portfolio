Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Hook Tabs is a Chrome extension designed to help users search and manage their browser tabs, bookmarks, and recently closed tabs.

Information Accessed

Hook Tabs accesses the following Chrome browser data solely to provide its functionality:

  • Open browser tabs, including tab titles and URLs
  • Bookmarks
  • Recently closed tabs and sessions

This information is processed locally in the user's browser and is used only to provide search, navigation, and tab management features.

Data Collection

Hook Tabs does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal or browsing data.

Hook Tabs does not use:

  • Analytics
  • Advertising
  • Tracking technologies
  • External servers
  • Third-party data collection services

No browsing data or user information is sent to the developer or any external service.

Data Storage

Hook Tabs does not store users' browsing data on external servers.

Extension settings, such as the selected language or user-configured keyboard shortcut, may be stored locally using Chrome's extension storage mechanisms.

Third-Party Services

Hook Tabs does not use third-party services to collect or process user data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the functionality of Hook Tabs changes. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
